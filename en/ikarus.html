Architecture

The data path from aircraft to analysis

The aircraft connects to the GCS over MAVLink; the GCS forwards raw packets to the pilot's phone and uploads the log to the team platform when the flight ends. On the Interceptor Drone, the lock-on module talks to the referee server.

Red: telemetry · green: commands · blue: flight log · yellow: lock-on
